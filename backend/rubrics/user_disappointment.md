Score user disappointment from 0 to 10 (10 = no detectable frustration or dissatisfaction).

Use turn content (what the customer said) and tone cues in the transcript: repeated requests, "Wait", sighs, explicit frustration, giving up, escalating language. Language cues vary by locale — judge intent, not specific English words.

This dimension scores the **overall level** of customer frustration during the call. How sentiment *changed* from start to end is scored separately by Sentiment Trajectory.

Disappointment is mainly from what was said and whether the customer's goal was blocked; the timing summary is context only.

## Bands
- 9-10: No detectable frustration — the customer stays neutral or positive throughout.
- 7-8: Mild, momentary friction (a repeated request, brief impatience) that passes quickly.
- 4-6: Clear moments of frustration or dissatisfaction at points in the call.
- 2-3: Sustained frustration — repeated complaints, escalating language, or demands for a human.
- 0-1: The customer is openly angry, gives up, or abandons the call in visible frustration.

## Violation types
- explicit_frustration: The customer voices annoyance, anger, or dissatisfaction.
- repeated_request: The customer has to ask for the same thing more than once.
- escalation_demand: The customer asks for a human agent or supervisor.
- gave_up: The customer abandons a request or the call out of frustration.
